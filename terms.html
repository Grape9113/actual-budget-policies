Purpose and availability

This application is a private, non-commercial integration between Enable Banking and my private Actual Budget instance hosted on PikaPods. I use it exclusively to manage my personal finances. It is not offered to the public, and no public access or service is provided.

The application retrieves account balances and transactions from my own bank accounts through Enable Banking. Access to banking information is authorized by me through my bank as part of the bank authorization process.

Financial data is stored in my private Actual Budget instance hosted on PikaPods for personal budgeting and financial management. I do not sell this data or use it for advertising. The Privacy Policy describes how the integration uses this information.

Third-party services

The integration depends on my bank, Enable Banking, Actual Budget, and PikaPods. Their applicable terms continue to apply to my use of those services. Availability and data retrieval depend on those services and my bank authorization.
